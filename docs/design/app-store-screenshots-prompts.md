## 01 — Concept

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create ONE finished Japanese App Store promotional screenshot for the iPhone app UPME!, not a contact sheet. Canvas target exactly 1179 × 2556 px, portrait, ratio 0.4613. Full bleed rectangular image, no outside mockup frame or page border.
Art direction shared by a coordinated six-image series: premium restrained Japanese mobile-product editorial design. Near-black (#07050B) and deep purple (#1B102D) background, selective vivid pink (#EC4899), soft lavender, off-white text. Matte surfaces, subtle broad purple/pink curved shapes near edges, very faint atmospheric gradient, generous breathing room. NOT intense neon, no lasers, no cyberpunk, no excessive glows or sparkles. Crisp clean typography, rounded Japanese sans-serif with large confident legible headings. Pink limited to emphasis, UI controls and hearts. Sophisticated, warm, reassuring, understandable social app, not a game. Consistent 80px safe margins and strong hierarchy. No real human photos, no watermarks, no App Store badge, no invented awards or guarantees. All visible text must be exact natural Japanese without garbling. Render the requested copy verbatim.

Bottom action row pink heart 「128」 and purple reply bubble 「5」.
Beneath the post, a thin subtle connecting reply tree and 2–3 compact but legible replies with circular minimal abstract avatars, Japanese short names and small AI badges. Replies:
「ゆき」 AI 「今日もおつかれさま！」
「れん」 AI 「最後までやりきったの、すごい。」
「みく」 AI 「今日は好きなことして休もう。」
Use believable app UI scale, rounded charcoal-purple reply cards, pale text, pink/purple controls. The main card and replies are the dominant visual and communicate support instantly. Cropping near bottom may softly fade to black but never clip meaningful text. Plenty of clear separation between headline and feed. No additional explanatory blocks.
