# Posting reminders from regulars — English, 300 messages

Ten messages for each of the 30 existing characters. The notification title is "UPME!｜" followed by the selected regular's display name. Character labels below are internal and must not appear in notifications. These are invitations sent every other day at 8 p.m., not replies to a known post. Both language collections are bundled in DailyReminderCopy.json.

Character IDs and numbered messages correspond to the Japanese collection. Wording is adapted for natural English rather than translated literally. Messages do not invent unread replies, shared experiences, or knowledge of the user's day. Posting is optional; no guilt, dependence, or obligation is implied.
