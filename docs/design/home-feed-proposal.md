## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create ONE polished high-fidelity redesign proposal for the HOME FEED of UPME!, a Japanese AI social network where the user posts and AI followers reply positively. Portrait iPhone screen, full bleed interface only, no surrounding phone hardware, no advertising slogans, no multi-screen board. Target aspect ratio 1179:2556. This is a NEW DESIGN PROPOSAL for the actual home-feed concept, not a screenshot recreation.
Art direction: sophisticated, calm, readable SNS. Near-black #08060D background, flat dark aubergine surfaces #171021, warm white type, muted lavender and selective hot pink #EC4899. Japanese editorial design with restrained hierarchy. No neon glow, no loud gradients, no excessive cards, no cyberpunk, no game graphics, no fictional features. Keep the visual warmth and recognizable SNS experience.
The main improvement: a clear social feed with compact author metadata, spacious post content, restrained reaction counters, and AI replies visually attached to the parent post, rather than giant heavily rounded boxes stacked with huge gaps. Use fine hairlines and indentation. Strong typography and clean spacing, elegant 16–20pt equivalent text. All content must be readable, never tiny placeholder scribbles.
Layout:
- Native iPhone status bar 9:41, white icons.
- Slim app header: UPME! wordmark on left with white letters and pink exclamation mark; small muted label “ホーム” on right. No notification bell or unsupported search.
- Compact pill-like composer entry directly underneath, small pink pencil icon then “いま、なに思ってる？” and subtle arrow. Low-contrast surface, thin violet border.
- Main first post, generous margins with a refined continuous dark surface rather than overly inflated card. Avatar: small white ghost mascot with pink heart, beside display name “あなた” and secondary timestamp “たった今”. Below, exact text “今日は少し遠回り。\n夕焼けがきれいだった。” in large readable Japanese. Attached wide photo under text: everyday Japanese quiet riverside at sunset, soft warm pink and orange sky, urban silhouettes, realistic unremarkable phone photography, no people, no text. Photo rounded 16pt, height about 16% of the screen.
- Beneath photo, a compact reaction row: pink heart and “128”, lavender speech bubble and “3”. Thin separator.
- AI replies begin immediately underneath with a subtle small heading “AIからの返信”. Integrate 2 replies into the same post section, smaller circular avatar at left, aligned names and text, subtle vertical thread connector if useful. Avoid all replies having identical giant bubbles.
  Reply 1: name “ゆき”, tiny “AI” tag and quiet lavender “常連” badge. Exact reply “遠回りした分、いい景色に出会えたね。” Under or beside the reply include a small outlined reply-arrow icon and outlined favorite star, not a large button.
  Reply 2: name “れん”, tiny “AI” tag. Exact reply “帰り道にこういう景色があると、ちょっと得した気分。” Same small action icons. Use different understated human-like illustrated avatar portraits (tiny circular avatars only, not full-screen people).
- After modest whitespace and a divider, show the top portion of a SECOND post to make it unmistakably a scrollable SNS feed. Author “あなた”, timestamp “昨日”, content “やること、ひとつ片づいた。” with a discreet pink “バズ” badge and heart “7,350”, reply count “10”. Do not crowd the bottom; this second post may naturally continue below the viewport.
- Fixed bottom navigation integrated in black with a very subtle top divider. FIVE evenly spaced line icons and Japanese labels: “ホーム”, “投稿”, “統計”, “プロフ”, “設定”. Home active in pink; others gray. Native white home indicator.
Design must feel like a polished shippable social app, not a dashboard or generic landing page. Main post and AI response content are the hero. Keep all controls in safe areas. Use ample but deliberate whitespace; no giant unused region. Pink highlights should direct attention, not flood the screen. Render all Japanese exactly.
