## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a genuinely elevated, visually rich HOME SCREEN design proposal for UPME!, a Japanese personal AI social network. ONE single portrait iPhone interface only, no phone hardware, no outer poster. Screen ratio 1179:2556. All UI should look sophisticated, designed, tactile, premium, and feasible in a real SwiftUI app.
IMPORTANT: This must be a substantial redesign, not a generic flat Twitter feed, not a plain list separated by lines, not another utilitarian wireframe. The user wants beautiful and rich UI especially. Still immediately understandable as YOUR POST plus AI REPLIES. Preserve black / deep purple / pink brand. No cyan, no neon beams, no galaxy, no fake game elements.

Visual direction: high-end editorial social app with restrained layered depth, rich dark plum surfaces, very delicate edge highlights, subtle material sheen on selected rounded surfaces, elegant thin-line icons, large carefully balanced Japanese typography, generous micro-spacing, asymmetric photo composition inside an orderly feed. Rounded outer post surfaces with smaller subordinate reply surfaces; beautiful intentional nested components rather than a wall of unrelated boxes. Sophisticated warm pink highlights and lavender secondary accents. Soft shadows, restrained glow ONLY on active primary control. Foreground text crisp and readable. Near black background with a very faint broad aubergine gradient. Not a glossy 3D toy app.

Detailed layout:
Native status bar with 9:41, white icons.
Header: UPME! typographic wordmark on left, pink exclamation; a small circular white ghost mascot avatar with a pink heart on the right. Below header a beautiful compact posting-entry capsule: pencil icon inset in a small pink-tinted circle on left, text “いま、なに思ってる？” in muted warm white, small lavender diagonal arrow on right. Elegant modest surface contrast. Make this feel like a premium control, not a huge form.
The hero is one photo post, in a dark plum rounded container, almost full width, with 20–24pt side margins. At top compact circular white ghost avatar, author “あなた”, timestamp “たった今”. Below text “今日は、少しだけ遠回り。” in large white type and secondary line “こんな夕焼けに会えるなら、悪くない。”.
Next a generous cinematic image of a Japanese riverside at sunset, warm muted mauve/orange dusk, beautiful ordinary phone photograph with river reflection and distant city buildings, no people. The photo fills the card's available width with nicely rounded corners. The image should be the visual anchor but not consume the entire screen.
Over the LOWER EDGE of the photo, a small tasteful dark floating pill with thin glass-like rim: pink heart + “128” and lavender comment outline + “3”. This is a refined functional reaction bar, not a flamboyant effect. It should overlap within the photo/card boundary, never hide the caption.
Immediately below within the SAME outer post container, an attached AI-replies area on a subtly darker surface:
Section heading “届いた返信” small and readable. Two tiny overlapping AI avatars on the right act as a reply-participant summary.
First reply: small tasteful circular portrait avatar, name “ゆき”, a compact lavender “常連” badge and tiny AI marker. A soft inset reply bubble with exact text “遠回りした分、いい景色に出会えたね。” in clean white. Minimal small reply arrow and filled pink star controls aligned to the right/bottom, legible targets.
Second reply: another small distinct circular portrait, name “れん”, tiny AI marker, exact text “この空、帰り道のごほうびだね。” Softer thinner bubble surface. Small reply arrow and outlined star. Maintain clear indentation and breathing room. No extra invented comments or controls.
After a short 24pt gap, show a SECOND compact post partly visible, dark card with slender pink accent stroke and a small beautifully designed “バズ” capsule. Author “あなた”, “昨日”; content “今日も一日、仕事がんばった！”; pink heart count “7,350” and lavender reply count “10”. This establishes scrolling and shows a second visual state without distracting from the main post.
Bottom navigation: a carefully proportioned floating dark charcoal dock with a subtle light border and medium rounded corners, inset from sides and safe-area bottom. Five equally spaced items “ホーム”, “投稿”, “統計”, “プロフ”, “設定”. Home active in pink with a small pill-shaped tinted background; other icons elegant thin-line gray. The 投稿 icon can be a compact outlined plus inside a rounded square but not a huge FAB. Home indicator below.
Keep usable touch target sizes, calm alignments, clear reading order, and realistic mobile density. Main post AND two replies must be visible above the dock; second post can be partly cropped by viewport. This should look like a premium finished product screenshot from a design studio. Render all Japanese copy accurately. No marketing text, no dashboard metrics, no search or notification features, no filters or feed tabs.
