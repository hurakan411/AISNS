## Prompt

Use case: ui-mockup.
Create a polished high-fidelity DESIGN PROPOSAL for the post composer of UPME!, a Japanese AI social network. ONE single portrait iPhone app screen, edge-to-edge UI only, no surrounding phone hardware, no marketing headline outside the UI, no multi-screen board. Portrait ratio 1179:2556. This is a proposed redesigned interface, not a reproduction of an existing screenshot.
Brand and art direction: restrained near-black #08060D, deep aubergine #171021, warm white text, muted lavender and selective hot pink #EC4899. Modern Japanese mobile editorial minimalism, thoughtful proportions, beautiful negative space, exceptionally readable. No neon glow, no gaming aesthetic, no glossy glassmorphism, no large purple panels, no decoration for its own sake.
The composer should feel like a quiet, inviting space to write, clearly still a social media app. Preserve simple functionality: text, one attached photo, remove photo, add photo, submit post. Do NOT invent voice, AI writing assistants, audience selectors, hashtags, scheduling or draft controls.
Layout from top to bottom:
1. Native iPhone status bar 9:41 with white status icons, black background. Comfortable safe areas.
2. Slim navigation header: a restrained x icon on the left, centered Japanese label “投稿” in medium 18pt equivalent white, no large NEW POST heading. Right side empty to preserve simplicity.
3. Below with generous but not excessive space, a compact identity row: 40pt circular white ghost mascot avatar with a small pink heart, name “あなた” medium bold, secondary label “AIフォロワーに届きます” in muted gray. A tiny subtle pink sparkle near that label is okay but no selector chevron.
4. Main open writing canvas directly on the background, NOT boxed in a big card. Large elegant white Japanese typography, realistic 22–24pt equivalent, ample leading. Exact user text on three lines:
“帰り道、少しだけ遠回り。
夕焼けがきれいで、
なんだか得した気分。”
Thin pink insertion cursor at end. Keep large comfortable outer margins.
5. One wide attached photo under the text with about 28pt vertical breathing room, rounded corners 20pt. Photo: understated beautiful warm late-afternoon sunset reflected in the windows of a quiet Japanese neighborhood, atmospheric everyday phone photography, no people, no readable store signs. Photo occupies about 22% of full screen height. Small black translucent circular x remove button at upper right. This is the only big boxed area.
6. Substantial calm writing space below. Very subtle purple ambient gradient in lower corner, almost imperceptible, not distracting.
7. Bottom action dock above the tab bar: left quiet outlined pill with picture icon and “写真を変更”; right prominent but tasteful rounded hot-pink button with small paper-plane outline icon and “投稿する” in white. Buttons have comfortable 48pt-equivalent height. Equal visual alignment, plenty of spacing. A small muted helper line immediately above this row reads “個人情報の投稿はお控えください”.
8. Bottom global navigation, dark integrated into background separated by a hairline: 5 equally spaced outline icons with small Japanese labels “ホーム”, “投稿”, “統計”, “プロフ”, “設定”. Only 投稿 active in pink, other icons gray. Native bottom home indicator.
Focus on strong composition and refined spacing, legibility at thumbnail scale, restrained color, elegant Japanese typography. Everything must fit inside one screen without cropping. No invented chat messages. No keyboard shown. Render all requested Japanese text accurately.
